Gold Annotator

Click words into rhyme groups; the tests/gold/-format annotation writes itself below. Shift-click extends the previous click into a multi-word span (same line).

Annotate groups, not pairs (“steady / ready / confetti — one family”) — the harness explodes groups into the pairs they imply. A group of A B C endorses A~C; if your ear rejects exactly that induced pair, veto it: enter veto mode and click the two words. Same-word repetition is not rhyme (rime riche) — don’t annotate door~door.
