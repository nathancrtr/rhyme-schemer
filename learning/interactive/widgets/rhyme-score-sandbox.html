Onsets are struck because the kernel ignores them — that’s what makes cat/hat rhyme. The nucleus dominates; the coda is texture (weight 0.35 vs 1.0).

Column score = (1.0·nucleus + 0.35·coda) / 1.35. A gap (extra syllable, no partner) scores 0 — a real rhyme cost. The final score is the column average.
